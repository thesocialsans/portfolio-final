Brief
Create an interactive Q&A ad experience for CoverMitra to address low engagement and high drop-offs in
traditional insurance ads by making information clearer, structured, and easier for users to understand and act on.
Why this matters?
Traditional insurance ads often rely on static information. 
They lack clarity, feel overwhelming, and fail to guide users, leading to
high drop-offs and low conversion.
Constrains
Interaction limited to click-only inputs

No typing, voice, or advanced interaction

Had to work within ad-format limitations

Designed for quick engagement and low attention span
